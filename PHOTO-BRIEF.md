# Photo brief

The site deliberately does not use generic stock business imagery. Add only approved images with a clear hospitality context.

1. **Founder portrait** — 1600×2000 px minimum, vertical. Environmental portrait in an actual hospitality setting, controlled natural light, no laptop pose, no folded-arms corporate cliché.
2. **Hotel operations** — 2000×1400 px minimum. Real service preparation, breakfast pass, briefing or guest-facing environment without staged handshake scenes.
3. **Restaurant operations** — 2000×1400 px minimum. Mise-en-place, pass communication, table detail or live service with genuine operational context.
4. **Bar operations** — 2000×1400 px minimum. Station setup, measured pouring, prep or cocktail production; avoid neon nightlife clichés.
5. **Training** — 2000×1400 px minimum. Small-group practical exercise with a real service station, menu or glassware rather than a classroom stock scene.
6. **Pre-opening** — 2000×1400 px minimum. Readiness check, floor plan / service-flow review, opening checklist or team briefing in the actual venue.

Export production media as AVIF with WebP fallback, always with explicit width/height. Keep individual above-the-fold media compact; the current hero intentionally uses text as LCP.
