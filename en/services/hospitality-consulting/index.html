Hospitality Consulting

When symptoms appear in several parts of an operation at once, an isolated measure is rarely enough. Consulting connects the guest view, shift routines, roles, F&B and available figures into a prioritised operating picture.

For whom

  • Hotels with recurring quality differences between shifts or seasons
  • Restaurants losing time at the interfaces between service, kitchen and leadership
  • Bars and new concepts that want to test operational decisions before larger investments

Operating problem

How the work is structured

The engagement starts by clarifying the reason for the assignment, the objective and the data available. Depending on scope, this is followed by observation of the guest journey and a live shift, conversations with key roles, and review of SOPs, rotas, POS reports or complaint patterns. Findings separate causes from symptoms. Measures are then ordered by impact, effort and ownership.

Operational depth

An engagement is not handled from a distance with a universal template. In a seasonal hotel, the same service rule may work in June and fail during peak week if handovers, language levels or role coverage are ignored. In a restaurant, a strong average spend can hide poor margin if product cost, portion discipline or sales mix is not reviewed. In a bar, a few seconds per drink can matter more across an evening than a new campaign.

Available data is therefore combined with observation. POS reports show what sold, but not automatically why. Checklists show what was intended, but not whether the sequence remains realistic during a full shift. Conversations with management and the team add context but do not replace watching the operation itself. The combination makes it possible to decide which measure should come first.

Implementation is documented so the operation can continue using it. Ownership, checkpoints and concise working documents are more valuable than a large presentation deck. Where a finding depends on an assumption, that assumption is stated. Where a figure is missing, it is not invented. This keeps the basis useful for later operational decisions.

Decisions in daily operations

In practice, the work also separates immediate corrections from structural issues. A weak dinner handover may be stabilised quickly through a concise briefing, while recurring uncertainty about roles requires a different intervention. Findings are therefore ranked by frequency, guest impact, commercial relevance and the degree to which management can influence them. This prevents the output from becoming a collection of opinions. Owners and managers receive a working picture that helps them decide what should be corrected now, what belongs after the season and what does not justify further effort.

FAQ

Hospitality Consulting

Do you need access to every KPI?

No. Work is based on the data available. Missing data is identified rather than replaced with false precision.

Can consulting work without long-term support?

Yes. A focused diagnostic engagement can be useful where the operation already has internal capacity to implement the findings.
