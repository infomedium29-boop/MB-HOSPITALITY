Pre-opening & Venue Development

Before opening, concept, purchasing, recruitment, technical work, training and the guest journey all move at the same time. Pre-opening work creates sequence, ownership and testing before the first real peak exposes gaps.

For whom

  • Investors and operators before a hotel, restaurant or bar opening
  • Existing operations preparing a relaunch, refurbishment or new F&B concept
  • Teams that need clear roles, standards and a training sequence before opening

Operating problem

How the work is structured

Starting from the intended opening date and working backwards, critical decisions, dependencies and test points are mapped. These can cover the offer, guest journey, roles, opening and closing routines, procurement, training plan, soft-opening scenarios and escalation routes. Not every decision requires a workshop; what matters is that open points are visible and owned.

Operational detail

Before opening, the greatest risk is usually not a lack of ideas but the wrong sequence of decisions. Training has limited value if the menu, POS setup or station layout changes again two days later, so dependencies and deadlines are made visible first.

The operating model is tested through concrete scenarios: group arrival, a sold-out item, a complaint, supplier delay, sudden bar pressure or a shift change. This exposes gaps in ownership and communication before normal trading begins.

Working documents, checklists and the training plan develop alongside testing. The goal is not to predict every exception, but to give the team stable base routines and clear escalation paths for opening.

Decisions in daily operations

Sequence matters during pre-opening. Training a service routine is inefficient if the menu, POS structure or station layout changes again two days later. Dependencies are therefore made visible: which decision must happen first, what can run in parallel and what requires a real soft-opening test. Critical guest scenarios can be rehearsed in advance, such as late arrival, a sold-out item, a complaint, a group arrival or sudden bar pressure. The objective is not to predict every exception, but to give the team stable base routines and clear escalation paths before normal trading begins.

Duration & format

Format: modular, from focused review to support across several pre-opening phases. The best start is before final time pressure arrives. Duration: depends on project scope.

FAQ

Pre-opening & Venue Development

When should pre-opening consulting begin?

Early enough for operational decisions to remain changeable. The exact start depends on venue size, build progress, team and concept maturity.

Do you manage all construction workstreams?

Only where explicitly included in scope. The hospitality focus is operations, F&B, service, team and opening readiness.
