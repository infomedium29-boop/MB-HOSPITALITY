Positioning

Consulting for operations that need practical clarity

FAQ

Working framework

Which languages can you work in?

Projects and training can be delivered in German, English and Croatian. The training language is matched to the team and operation.

Where do projects take place?

The main focus is Vorarlberg and the Alpine region. Depending on the assignment, work across DACH, Croatia and international locations is possible.

What happens in the initial call?

The first call covers the operation, reason for the enquiry, objective, timeframe and available information. This makes it possible to define a focused module or a broader engagement.

How are fees structured?

Fees are agreed according to the scope and format of each project. The commercial model, deliverables and costs are documented transparently before work begins.

How long does a project take?

Duration depends on the starting point, objective and scope. A realistic timeframe is confirmed after the initial assessment.

Remote or on site?

Analysis, documentation and coordination can partly happen remotely. Training, live observation and operational implementation may require work on site.

Next step
