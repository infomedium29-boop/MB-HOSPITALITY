Hosting

The site is prepared for hosting through Cloudflare Pages. Technical connection and security data may be processed when the site is requested. The production Cloudflare configuration must be checked against the applicable contractual and privacy settings before launch.

Contact form and Web3Forms

When the contact form is used, the submitted details are sent to Web3Forms for the purpose of handling the enquiry. These include name, email address, optional phone number, type of operation, message and consent status. The applicable legal basis and retention period must be confirmed before launch according to the final company and contractual setup.

Fonts and external content

Fonts are served locally by the website, so there is no request to Google Fonts. The map is not loaded on page view. Only after the visitor actively selects “Load map” is an OpenStreetMap iframe inserted; this can establish a connection to OpenStreetMap.

Cookies and analytics

This static version does not set tracking cookies. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, the final production configuration should be reviewed before publication. If additional analytics, marketing or embedded services are added later, this policy and, where required, the consent mechanism must be updated.
